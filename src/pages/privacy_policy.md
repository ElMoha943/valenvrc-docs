# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 20/12/2025

At ValenVRC (also known as "Vals Creations"), your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you access or use our services.

## Information We Collect
We collect the following types of information from users of our services:

- **Discord User ID**
- **VRChat User ID**
- **Unity Account Email**
- **VRChat World ID**
- **Assets Purchased from Our Store**
- **Date of Asset Use Initiation**
- **Purchase Source (e.g., Gumroad, Jinxxy)**

## Use of Information
We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- Granting access to systems and assets.
- Preventing unauthorized access and use.
- Conducting internal statistics or studies on product performance (e.g., which products sell more).

## Third-Party Sharing
We do not share your personal data with third parties, nor do we sell your data. However, third-party services that we use, such as Gumroad, Jinxxy, and Discord, may collect and process your data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies for more information.

## Data Retention
We retain your personal data indefinitely unless you request deletion. Upon deletion, any related services will cease, and we will stop processing your data. Certain data, such as license key hashes, may be retained to prevent unauthorized reuse.

## Security
Our database is hosted by Pebblehost. Please refer to their [Privacy Policy](https://pebblehost.com/legal) for more information about their security measures.

## Children's Privacy
Our assets are intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Contact Information
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us through our [Discord Server](https://valenvrc.com/discord).

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes by posting the updated Privacy Policy on our website.

---
By using our services, you consent to the collection and use of your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy.
