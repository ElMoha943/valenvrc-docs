## 4. Information We Collect and Store

We collect and store only the information needed to operate our licensing, validation, access control, anti-abuse, and support systems.

### 4.1 License Activation Records

When a license is activated through our Unity License Manager app or related licensing tools, we may store the following information:

* Product ID.
* SHA-256 hash of the license key.
* License type.
* Store or source of the license.
* VRChat user ID provided by the user.
* Unity account identifier provided by the user.
* VRChat world ID, when applicable.
* Activation status.
* Activation creation date and time.

We do not store the raw license key in these activation records. License keys are stored as SHA-256 hashes where applicable.

These records are used to activate licenses, verify legitimate access, prevent unauthorized license reuse, and enforce license restrictions.

### 4.2 Discord License Validation Records

When a license is activated or validated through a Discord bot, dashboard, or Discord-related licensing flow, we may store the following information:

* Discord user ID.
* Product ID.
* SHA-256 hash of the license key.
* Store or source of the license.
* Validation status.
* Validation creation date and time.

These records are used to grant or revoke access to Discord-based support areas, private channels, product-specific areas, or other license-restricted resources.

### 4.3 Discord OAuth and Discord API Data

When Discord OAuth is used to log in to a dashboard or related service, we may access limited Discord account information provided by Discord, such as:

* Discord account ID.
* Discord username.
* Discord display name.
* Other public Discord account information made available through the Discord API.

We may use this information to identify the account, manage dashboard access, verify permissions, and grant access to private support channels or license-restricted areas.

We do not use Discord OAuth data for advertising, profiling, or selling user data.

### 4.4 Purchase and Storefront Information

Purchases are made through third-party storefronts such as **Gumroad** and **Jinxxy**. Those platforms process purchases under their own terms and privacy policies.

We use the information those storefronts provide to us only as needed to verify purchases, validate licenses, provide access to assets, process support requests, and prevent license abuse.

We do not send end-user personal information to Gumroad or Jinxxy for our own independent purposes. However, those platforms may collect and process information directly from users when users purchase through them.

### 4.5 Support Information

If you contact us for support, privacy requests, license help, bug reports, or other assistance, we may process the information you provide, such as:

* Your email address.
* Discord ID or username, if provided.
* VRChat ID, if provided.
* License key or license-related information.
* Product name or product ID.
* Details of the issue or support request.
* Any files, screenshots, logs, or messages you choose to provide.

We use this information only to respond to the request, verify license ownership, investigate issues, and provide support.

### 4.6 Technical and Server Information

Our services may generate technical information through normal operation, including backend logs, server logs, security logs, request timestamps, error logs, and related operational records.

Depending on the service or provider, this information may include technical data such as IP addresses, request metadata, browser or application information, endpoint activity, or error details.

Server and infrastructure logs are retained according to our provider defaults or operational needs, unless longer retention is required for security, abuse prevention, debugging, legal compliance, or service integrity.

## 8. License Records and Retention

License activation and validation records are retained for as long as our licensing system remains active and we continue operating the relevant products or services.

This retention is necessary to prevent license reuse, unauthorized sharing, abuse, fraud, and repeated activation of the same license by unrelated users.

Because license records are used to enforce product access and prevent abuse, deleting or unlinking license-related records may cause the license to stop working.

## 9. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion or unlinking of their license-related data by contacting:

**[support@valenvrc.com](mailto:support@valenvrc.com)**

To process a deletion request, we may require enough information to verify ownership and locate the correct record.

For VRChat/Unity license activations, you may need to provide:

* License key.
* VRChat ID.

For Discord license validations, you may need to provide:

* License key.
* Discord ID.

If a deletion request is approved and processed, we may remove or unlink the VRChat ID, Discord ID, Unity account identifier, or other account-specific data from the relevant license record.

However, deletion or unlinking may make the license unusable because the connection between the license and the authorized account will be removed. In some cases, we may also retain minimal records necessary to prevent fraud, abuse, license reuse, chargeback abuse, or unauthorized reactivation.

Deletion requests will be processed within a reasonable time.
