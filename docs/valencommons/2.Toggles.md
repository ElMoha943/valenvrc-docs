A selection of scripts to toggle various things on and off.

### Trigger Toggle

Toggles the active state of a GameObject when a player enters a trigger collider. Can be used by attaching a Collider with "Is Trigger" enabled.

- ToggleableObjects : GameObject[] - The GameObjects to toggle.

:::note
Only works for local player.
:::
