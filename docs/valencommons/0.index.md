You can install valencommons from my [VPM Listing](https://valenvrc.com/), just click the "Add To VCC" button or manually copy the link and put it into your Creator Companion or ALCOM.

![Package Listing](image.png)

![Package in ALCOM](image-1.png)

You will find it on the **Packages** folder (not ASSETS). You will find the scripts and prefabs on
`Packages/Valenvrc - Common/Runtime`.
