* **Import ValenCommons from my [public VCC listing](https://valenvrc.com/)⚠️**
* Import RoomSystem.unitypackage
* Locate the RoomSystem prefab inside `Packages/Valenvrc - RoomSystem/Runtime/` and drag it to your scene.
* Follow this wiki instructions to customize it to your liking.

## Setting Up a Room

The default RoomSystem prefab comes with a sample room already setup, you can use it as reference to create your own rooms. To create a new room:

1. Duplicate the `Room1` template found inside the `Rooms` GameObject.
![alt text](/img/docs/room-system/room-duplicate.png)
2. Add the new room to the `Rooms` array in the `RoomSystem` component.
3. Resize the room collider to fit the whole area of your room.
