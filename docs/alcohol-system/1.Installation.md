:::info
This section refers to the AlcoholSystem 2.0 beta, some content may differ from your current version.
:::

* **Import ValenCommons from my [public VCC listing](https://valenvrc.com/)⚠️**
* Import AlcoholSystem.unitypackage
* Locate the AlcoholSystem prefab inside `Packages/Valenvrc - AlcoholSystem/Runtime/` and drag it to your scene.
* Follow this wiki instructions to customize it to your liking.
