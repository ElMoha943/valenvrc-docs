## Installation

* Import ValenCommons from my [public VCC listing](https://valenvrc.com/)⚠️
* Import SecurityKeypad.unitypackage
* Locate the SecurityKeypad prefab inside `Packages/Valenvrc - SecurityKeypad/Runtime/SecurityKeypad` and drag it to your scene.
* Follow this wiki instructions to customize it to your liking.
