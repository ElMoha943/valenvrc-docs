# Installation

- Find the prefab on `Packages/valenvrc - Elevator/Runtime` and drag it to your scene.
- Duplicate one of the existing **Elevator Floors** and move it to the desired height for your next floor.
- Add the newly created floor to the **Elevator System** component's **Floors** list.
