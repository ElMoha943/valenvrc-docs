:::note

:::

* **Import ValenCommons from my [public VCC listing](https://valenvrc.com/)⚠️**
* Import AdminTablet.unitypackage
* Locate the AdminTablet prefab inside `Packages/Valenvrc - AdminTablet/Runtime/AdminTablet` and drag it to your scene.
* Follow this wiki instructions to customize it to your liking.

:::danger[Important Considerations:]

* NEVER turn off the tablet gameobject or their modules, they are all needed for the tablet to work properly.
* Keep all modules as childs of the main Tablet script or they won't be able to find the reference to it for notifications and permission checks.

:::
